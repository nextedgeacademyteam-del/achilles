Achilles privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026.

Achilles is a private tool used only by its owner, Abhishek Pandey, for his own YouTube channels.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, Achilles reads YouTube channel data and YouTube Analytics for the owner's channels, reads public comments on the owner's videos, and posts replies to those comments as the owner's channel.

How data is used

Data is used only to show the owner how his videos perform and to reply to viewers. It is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Comment text may be processed by an AI model to draft a reply.

Storage and deletion

Sign-in tokens and analytics are stored on the owner's own computer and server. Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's Security settings, under third-party access.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Achilles uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
